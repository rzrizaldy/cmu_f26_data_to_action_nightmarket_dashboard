    The schedule

    In date order. Added visitors = predicted lift × a typical Saturday evening there in that month.

      How the model picks

      1. Predict the lift. Past markets in 7 host neighborhoods raised evening footfall by −15% to +54%. A ridge regression links that lift to population, businesses, bus stops and spending, and predicts it everywhere else.
      2. Turn lift into people. Lift × the neighborhood's usual Saturday evening crowd gives added visitors per market.
      3. Pick the best schedule that follows the rules. Gurobi searches every neighborhood–Saturday combination and proves no better schedule exists.
